# The Last Beacon — production plan

An original, wordless 48-second animated short by ZJUCQR. A solitary mechanical keeper repairs a celestial beacon. A distant light answers. The emotional arc is solitude, attention, effort, wonder, connection.

## Art direction

A deliberately stylized miniature world: midnight blue, porcelain ivory, brushed brass, teal light and restrained amber highlights. The keeper has an oversized rounded faceplate, articulated limbs, separate fingers, inset luminous eyes, layered ceramic armor, exposed metal joints and a wind-responsive red scarf. Consistent geometry and a continuous performance clock span every cut. No generated photographs, downloaded characters or voice impersonation.

## Shot and sound plan

| Time | Picture | Sound |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–8 s | Slow approach to an observatory above the clouds | Wind, sparse felt-piano motif, low strings |
| 8–16 s | Keeper portrait: head tilt, eye response, breathing | Close mechanical hum, glass overtones |
| 16–24 s | Arm extends; fingers meet the instrument at 20 s | Servo movement, contact click, rising resonance |
| 24–32 s | Orbital rings open around a luminous core | Tonal ignition at 24 s, strings and piano bloom |
| 32–40 s | The beacon sends a constellation into the sky | Bell cascade and spatial signal at 32 s |
| 40–48 s | Distant lights answer; keeper looks up | Answer motif at 40 s, warm resolution, fading air |

Delivery: 1920 × 1080, 24 fps, stereo 48 kHz AAC, H.264. Blender EEVEE with volumetric clouds, soft lighting, depth of field, AgX and restrained bloom. Sound is original procedural synthesis, with separate music, ambience and effects stems, a shared event clock and measured loudness. This is a designed animated film, not a claim of live-action photorealism.

## Review gates

Render representative frames before committing to the master. Inspect silhouettes, hand contact, face readability, shot continuity, lighting and framing. Then inspect a contact sheet and animated excerpts, decode the complete master, verify frame count and audio duration, and measure audio loudness and true peak. Publish only the finished MP4 as a release asset; keep intermediates under ignored `out/`.
